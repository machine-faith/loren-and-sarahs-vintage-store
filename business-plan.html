The Deeper Prompts

Click any card to read guiding questions. Your answers will sync with the Brand Studio above.

🏷️ What's the Name?

+

This is blocking your domain, Instagram bio, packaging, and custom stickers.

  • Does it need to say "vintage", or can it be abstract like Women's History Museum?
  • Does it roll off the tongue? Text it to three friends with zero context. What do they imagine?
  • Does it capture both the antique vintage fabric AND the cold metallic chainmail?
  • Could it reference the masks, the theater, the archival nature of the past?
Rule of Thumb Don't let perfection hold back launching. If you find a name you 80% love and the .com or .co is open, claim it.

💰 What Are You Actually Selling?

+

WHM operates two storefronts: Vintage Store ($50–$1,300) and Boutique (original designs). You have an even richer mix:

  • Curated Vintage: Clothing, rare lingerie, jackets, skirts, 70s-90s designer.
  • Handmade Chainmail: Loren's armor-jewelry — high margins, zero sourcing constraints.
  • Jewelry & Metalwork: Sarah's craft, utilizing jewelry store trade knowledge.
  • Historical Ephemera: Antique hair combs, buttons, vintage matchbooks, postcards.

🎯 Who is the Customer?

+
  • The Depop Customer: Fast-moving, 18–26, searching for specific aesthetics, responsive to video styling.
  • The Collector / Art Crowd: Buys through website lookbooks, attends gallery openings, appreciates Lily's masks and Loren's chainmail craftsmanship ($200+ ticket items).
  • Local vs International: Chainmail is lightweight and cheap to post worldwide; coats/boots take up volume.

📸 The Masked / Anonymous Shoots

+

The anonymity angle is brilliant. Why?

  • It shifts focus completely to the garment, the chainmail, and the sculptural mask.
  • Anyone can be the model without needing hair & makeup artist overhead.
  • It gives the brand an uncanny, high-fashion art-object aura (think Maison Margiela, WHM, Leigh Bowery).
Shooting Tip Shoot both horizontal landscape for the website hero slideshow, and portrait 3:4 for the product grid and Depop listings.

Blind Spots to Pre-empt

Things that surprise new vintage & jewelry sellers.

📦 Measuring, Storage & Care

+

Vintage sizing tags (e.g. "Vintage Size 12") mean nothing today. Always provide exact flat measurements: bust, waist, hips, length. For chainmail jewelry, note the circumference and whether it has an adjustable extender chain.

⚖️ Custom Chainmail Orders

+

People will inevitably ask Loren: "Can you make this chainmail collar in gold or custom size for my neck?" Decide now if you offer made-to-order sizing (great markup opportunity) and require 50% deposit upfront.
